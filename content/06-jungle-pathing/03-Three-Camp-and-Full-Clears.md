# Three Camp and Full Clears

Junglers do not always clear the same number of camps before interacting with other players. Two basic ideas are a shorter clear and a full clear.

## Three-Camp Clear

A three-camp clear means defeating three jungle camps before moving toward another play.

This can create an earlier opportunity to:

- Gank.
- Enter the river.
- Contest an enemy.
- Help a teammate.

However, the jungler leaves more camps available.

## Full Clear

A full clear focuses on defeating most or all available camps before making another play.

Possible advantages include:

- More gold.
- More experience.
- Predictable progression through camps.

The disadvantage is that the jungler may reach lanes later.

> Clearing more camps provides resources, while clearing fewer camps may create earlier map pressure.

The correct decision depends on the champion and what is happening during the match.
