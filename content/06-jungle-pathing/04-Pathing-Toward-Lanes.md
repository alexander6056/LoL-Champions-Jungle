# Pathing Toward Lanes

A jungler can plan their route so that the final camp places them near a specific lane. This makes jungle pathing directly connected to [[01-jungle-basics/04-Ganking-Basics|Ganking]].

## Choosing a Lane

Before pathing toward a lane, consider:

- Champion matchups.
- Enemy position.
- Ally health.
- Enemy health.
- Enemy escape abilities.
- Possible enemy vision.

A jungler should not automatically gank the lane just because their route finishes nearby. The situation may change while camps are being cleared.

## Example

A simple route may look like:

Jungle Camp &rarr; Jungle Camp &rarr; Jungle Camp &rarr; Check Nearby Lane

If the gank is good, the jungler can attack. If the gank is poor, the jungler can continue farming or move somewhere else.

> A jungle route creates an opportunity, but the jungler still has to decide whether the opportunity is good.
