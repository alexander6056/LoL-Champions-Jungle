# Adapting Your Path

A jungler may begin the game with a planned route, but the route should not always remain the same. League of Legends constantly changes as players move around the map.

## Reasons to Change a Path

A jungler may change their route because:

- An enemy is very low on health.
- A teammate needs help.
- The enemy jungler appears nearby.
- An objective becomes available.
- A jungle camp was taken.
- A planned gank is no longer possible.

## Making Decisions

Changing the route does not mean the original plan was bad. It means the jungler is responding to new information. The player should compare the value of continuing the original route with the value of changing it.

> Jungle pathing is a plan that should change when better information becomes available.
