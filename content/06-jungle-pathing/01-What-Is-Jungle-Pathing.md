# What Is Jungle Pathing?

Jungle pathing is the route a jungler follows while moving between jungle camps and other areas of the map. A path is not only about deciding which monsters to defeat. The route should also place the jungler in a useful location afterward.

## Why Pathing Matters

Pathing affects:

- Gold.
- Experience.
- Gank opportunities.
- Objective control.
- Position on the map.
- Encounters with the enemy jungler.

For example, a jungler may choose a path that finishes near a lane they want to gank.

Another path may finish close to an important objective.

> Good pathing combines efficient farming with useful map positioning.
