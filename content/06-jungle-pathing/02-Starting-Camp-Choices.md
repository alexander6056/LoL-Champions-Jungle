# Starting Camp Choices

The first jungle camp a player chooses can affect where they finish their first clear. A jungler should think about their plan before the camps begin spawning.

## Things to Consider

Before choosing a starting camp, consider:

- Which camps the champion clears efficiently.
- Which lane may need help.
- Where the enemy jungler may begin.
- Which side of the map contains an important objective.
- Whether the jungler wants a short or long clear.

Starting on one side of the map normally causes the jungler to move toward the opposite side.

## Planning Ahead

A starting camp should be connected to a larger plan.

For example:

Start &rarr; Clear Camps &rarr; Reach Lane &rarr; Gank or Continue Farming

> The first camp is the beginning of the jungle route, not an isolated decision.

The next decision involves choosing how many camps to clear before making a play.
