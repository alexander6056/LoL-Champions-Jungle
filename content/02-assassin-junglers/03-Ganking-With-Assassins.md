# Ganking With Assassins

Ganking is especially important for assassin junglers because their damage and mobility can make them dangerous when approaching a lane unexpectedly.

The general ideas from [[01-jungle-basics/04-Ganking-Basics|Ganking Basics]] still apply.

## Before Ganking

An assassin should check:

1. Enemy health.
2. Allied champion health.
3. Enemy position.
4. Possible enemy vision.
5. Available abilities.
6. Possible escape routes.
7. Location of the enemy jungler.

Assassins usually benefit from attacking enemies who have moved far away from their tower.

## Using Surprise

Approaching from an area without enemy vision can make it harder for the enemy to react.

An assassin does not always need to get a kill. Forcing an enemy to retreat or use an important ability can still help a teammate.

> A successful assassin gank depends on choosing the right opportunity instead of attacking every available lane.

After a successful gank, the jungler may be able to rotate toward an [[01-jungle-basics/05-Objectives|Objective]].
