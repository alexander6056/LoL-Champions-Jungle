# Assassin Jungle Champions

Several champions can demonstrate the assassin jungle playstyle.

Examples include:

- Kha'Zix
- Rengar
- Evelynn
- Shaco
- Talon

Individual champions have different abilities, but they generally share some assassin characteristics.
## Common Characteristics

Assassin junglers often have:

- Strong burst damage.
- Mobility or movement abilities.
- Ways to approach enemies unexpectedly.
- Strong damage against isolated targets.
- Lower durability than tanks.

For example, one assassin may depend heavily on stealth while another may use mobility to quickly enter a fight.

> Champion abilities may be different, but the basic assassin goal remains finding and eliminating vulnerable targets.

Learning the general assassin playstyle first makes it easier to understand individual champions.
