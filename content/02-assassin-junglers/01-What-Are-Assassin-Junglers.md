# What Are Assassin Junglers?

Assassin junglers are champions designed to quickly attack and eliminate important enemy targets. They usually depend on high damage, mobility, and surprise instead of having large amounts of health or defensive abilities.

Assassins commonly look for enemy champions who are alone or out of position.

## Main Goals

An assassin jungler normally wants to:

- Farm enough gold and experience to remain strong.
- Look for vulnerable enemy champions.
- Create pressure through [[03-Ganking-With-Assassins|ganks]].
- Avoid unnecessary extended fights.
- Attack important enemy damage dealers during team fights.

Assassins often become dangerous when they are able to approach enemies without being seen.

> Assassin junglers rely on timing and positioning more than durability.

Understanding the normal [[01-jungle-basics/01-What-Is-Jungling|Jungle Role]] is important because assassins still need to balance farming, objectives, and helping teammates.
