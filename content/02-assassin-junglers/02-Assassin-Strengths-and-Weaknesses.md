# Assassin Strengths and Weaknesses

Assassin junglers can be extremely dangerous when they gain an advantage, but their high damage usually comes with several weaknesses.

## Strengths

Common assassin strengths include:

- High burst damage.
- Strong mobility.
- Good ability to attack isolated enemies.
- Strong ganking potential.
- Ability to pressure enemy damage dealers.
- Good ability to move around the map.

Their mobility may allow them to enter or leave fights faster than many other champions.

## Weaknesses

Assassins may struggle because they often have:

- Lower durability.
- Difficulty fighting tanks.
- Problems when they fall behind in gold.
- Greater dependence on positioning.
- Difficulty during long team fights.

An assassin that attacks at the wrong time may be defeated before they can eliminate their target.

> High damage does not always mean a champion should attack immediately.

These strengths and weaknesses influence how a player approaches [[03-Ganking-With-Assassins|Ganking With Assassins]].
