# Objectives

Objectives are important map targets that help a team build advantages in League of Legends. For a jungler, neutral objectives are especially important because they are located outside the normal lanes and can be contested by both teams. Common major objectives include Dragons and Baron Nashor, along with other neutral monsters that can provide team advantages when secured.
## Preparing for an Objective

Before starting an objective, a jungler should consider the situation around the area. Useful questions include:

1. Are nearby teammates able to help?
2. Are enemy champions visible on the map?
3. Does the enemy team have vision around the objective?
4. Is [[03-Smite-and-Jungle-Items|Smite]] available?
5. Could clearing a nearby camp or attempting a [[04-Ganking-Basics|gank]] create a safer opportunity first?
### Connecting Objectives to Jungle Play

Objectives show why the jungle role is about more than simply defeating camps. A jungler may begin by clearing [[02-Jungle-Camps|Jungle Camps]], help a teammate in lane, and then use the resulting pressure to move toward an objective.

> Objective control is strongest when the team creates a safe opportunity before starting the fight.

Starting a major neutral objective without information can be risky because the enemy team may contest it. Vision, teammate position, and the location of the opposing jungler all matter.

This connects back to [[01-What-Is-Jungling|What Is Jungling?]]. Farming, ganking, Smite usage, and objective control are all parts of the same role. A player who understands how these pieces work together has a stronger foundation for learning more advanced jungle pathing and champion-specific strategies.
