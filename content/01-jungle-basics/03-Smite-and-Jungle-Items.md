# Smite and Jungle Items

Smite is the summoner spell most closely associated with the jungle role in League of Legends. It is used against neutral monsters and helps the jungler clear the jungle and contest important objectives. Because the jungler spends much of the early game fighting neutral monsters instead of lane minions, Smite is a major part of the role's basic tool set.
## Why Smite Matters

Smite helps the jungler in several ways:

- It supports clearing [[02-Jungle-Camps|Jungle Camps]].
- It gives the jungler a reliable way to damage neutral monsters.
- It helps secure major [[05-Objectives|Objectives]] when their health becomes low.
- It forces the jungler to think carefully about when to use or save the spell.

Jungle-specific starting items also support the role by improving the jungler's ability to fight neutral monsters and progress through the early game.
### Decision Making

Smite is not only about pressing a button on a monster. The jungler has to decide whether using it now is more valuable than saving it for another camp or an upcoming objective.

> Saving an important resource can be just as valuable as using it immediately.

This connects Smite to the rest of the jungle role. A jungler may clear camps, move into a [[04-Ganking-Basics|gank]], and then rotate toward an objective. Those actions are connected rather than separate. The basic ideas described in [[01-What-Is-Jungling|What Is Jungling?]] become easier to understand once the player sees how Smite, camps, ganks, and objectives all affect one another.
