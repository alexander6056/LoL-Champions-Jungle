# Jungle Camps
  
Jungle camps are groups of neutral monsters found in the jungle areas of Summoner's Rift. They are one of the main sources of gold and experience for the jungler. Unlike lane minions, jungle monsters stay in specific camp locations until they are defeated. Learning where the camps are located helps a new jungler understand how to move around the map and plan a basic clear.
## Common Camps

Several well-known jungle camps are used during normal jungle clearing:

1. Blue Sentinel
2. Red Brambleback
3. Gromp
4. Wolves
5. Raptors
6. Krugs

The exact order used to clear these camps depends on the champion, the player's plan, and what is happening in nearby lanes.

### Why Camp Order Matters

The route a jungler takes through camps is often called jungle pathing. A player may choose a route that ends near top lane, mid lane, bottom lane, or an important neutral objective. That decision can create an opportunity for a [[04-Ganking-Basics|gank]] or help the team prepare for [[05-Objectives|Objectives]].


> Efficient clearing is not only about killing camps quickly; it is also about finishing the clear in a useful part of the map.

  
  Jungle camps also connect directly to [[03-Smite-and-Jungle-Items|Smite and Jungle Items]]. Smite helps a jungler manage neutral monsters and is especially important when fighting over major objectives. Understanding camps is therefore one of the first practical skills needed after learning [[01-What-Is-Jungling|What Is Jungling?]].
