# Early, Mid, and Late Game

A League of Legends match changes as champions gain levels, gold, and items. The jungle role also changes as the match progresses.

## Early Game

During the early game, junglers commonly focus on:

- Clearing camps.
- Gaining levels.
- Ganking lanes.
- Tracking the enemy jungler.
- Contesting early objectives.

## Mid Game

During the mid game, players begin moving around the map more frequently.

The jungler may focus on:

- Team fights.
- Objectives.
- Map control.
- Catching isolated enemies.
- Continuing to farm.

## Late Game

During the late game, individual mistakes can become more important.

Junglers should pay close attention to:

- Major objectives.
- Team positioning.
- Enemy locations.
- Smite availability.
- Staying with teammates.

> Jungle priorities change throughout the match even though farming, pressure, and objectives remain important.
