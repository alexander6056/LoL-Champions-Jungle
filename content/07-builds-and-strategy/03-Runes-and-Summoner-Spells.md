# Runes and Summoner Spells

Runes provide additional effects and statistics that influence how a champion plays. Summoner spells provide abilities that are separate from the champion's normal ability kit.

## Runes

Rune choices can support different parts of a champion's playstyle.

They may improve:

- Damage.
- Durability.
- Movement.
- Healing.
- Utility.
- Scaling.

Different champions may prefer different rune setups.

## Summoner Spells

Junglers normally depend heavily on [[01-jungle-basics/03-Smite-and-Jungle-Items|Smite]].

Smite helps with:

- Jungle camps.
- Jungle progression.
- Neutral objectives.

The second summoner spell depends on the champion and strategy. Flash is commonly useful because it can help a champion engage, escape, or reposition.

> Runes and summoner spells should support what the champion needs to accomplish during the match.
