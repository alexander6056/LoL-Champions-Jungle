# Adapting to the Enemy Team

Jungle strategy should not remain exactly the same every game. The champions selected by the enemy team can affect pathing, builds, ganking, and team fighting.

## Questions to Ask

A jungler can ask:

1. Which enemies are easiest to gank?
2. Which enemy champions are dangerous during early fights?
3. Where might the enemy jungler path?
4. Does the enemy team have strong crowd control?
5. Does the enemy team deal mostly physical or magic damage?
6. Which enemy champion is the biggest threat?
7. Which objectives are most important right now?

## Changing the Plan

The jungler may need to change:

- [[06-jungle-pathing/05-Adapting-Your-Path|Jungle Pathing]]
- Item choices.
- Target selection.
- Ganking priorities.
- Team-fighting position.
- Objective decisions.

A strategy that works against one team may not work against another.

> Strong jungle play comes from understanding the fundamentals and adjusting them to the current match.

This brings together the concepts from [[01-jungle-basics/index|Jungle Basics]], champion classes, [[06-jungle-pathing/index|Jungle Pathing]], and builds into one complete jungle strategy.
