# Choosing Items

Items provide statistics and effects that improve a champion. Choosing the correct items can help the jungler perform their role more effectively.

## Champion Role

Different roles commonly value different statistics.

### Assassins

May value:

- Damage.
- Ability haste.
- Movement.
- Offensive effects.

### Fighters

May value:

- Damage.
- Health.
- Defensive statistics.
- Sustain.

### Tanks

May value:

- Health.
- Armor.
- Magic resistance.
- Utility.

### Mages

May value:

- Ability power.
- Ability haste.
- Mana when needed.
- Magic-focused effects.

## Adapting Items

The enemy team should also affect item choices. If the enemy team deals a large amount of one type of damage, defensive choices may become more valuable.

> The best item for a champion can change depending on the match.

This is why builds should be viewed as flexible rather than as an unchanging list.
