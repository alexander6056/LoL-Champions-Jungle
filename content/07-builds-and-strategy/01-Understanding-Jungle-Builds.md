# Understanding Jungle Builds

A build is the combination of items, runes, and other choices used to strengthen a champion during a match. Different champions need different builds because their abilities and roles are different.

## Parts of a Build

A jungle build may include:

- Jungle starting item.
- Boots.
- Damage items.
- Defensive items.
- Utility items.
- Runes.
- Summoner spells.

Builds should support what the champion is trying to accomplish. For example, an assassin usually values different statistics than a tank.

## Builds Can Change

A player does not always need to purchase exactly the same items every game. The enemy team and current game situation may change what is useful.

> A build should support the champion's role and respond to the situation in the match.
