# Fighter Jungle Champions

Many champions can represent the fighter jungle playstyle.

Examples include:

- Vi
- Lee Sin
- Xin Zhao
- Jarvan IV
- Warwick

These champions do not play exactly the same way, but they commonly combine damage with the ability to survive longer battles.

## Common Fighter Features

Fighter junglers may have:

- Gap-closing abilities.
- Crowd control.
- Healing or defensive abilities.
- Strong basic attacks.
- Sustained damage.

Some fighters are better at engaging enemies while others are stronger during duels.

> Fighter champions usually succeed by staying active in a fight rather than attacking once and immediately leaving.
