# Ganking With Fighters

Fighter junglers often use a mixture of damage and crowd control when ganking lanes.
The basic checks from [[01-jungle-basics/04-Ganking-Basics|Ganking Basics]] still apply.

## Starting a Gank

Before attacking, check:

- Enemy positioning.
- Ally positioning.
- Health levels.
- Available abilities.
- Enemy vision.
- Location of the enemy jungler.

Some fighter junglers can begin a gank using a movement or crowd-control ability.

Others may need to approach the enemy before using their strongest abilities.

## Staying in the Fight

Unlike many assassins, fighters may continue attacking after their first abilities have been used.
Their durability and sustained damage can make longer ganks effective.

> Fighter ganks often become extended battles instead of one quick attack.

A gank may also turn into a fight against the enemy jungler, leading to a [[04-Dueling-and-Skirmishing|skirmish]].
