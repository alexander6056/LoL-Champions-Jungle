# Fighter Strengths and Weaknesses

Fighter junglers are often flexible because they can provide both damage and durability.
However, they are not perfect in every situation.

## Strengths

Fighters may have:

- Good sustained damage.
- Moderate durability.
- Strong dueling ability.
- Useful ganking abilities.
- Strong small-team fighting.
- Ability to contest jungle areas.

These abilities make many fighters dangerous when encountering the enemy jungler.

## Weaknesses

Possible weaknesses include:

- Less burst damage than assassins.
- Less durability than tanks.
- Dependence on gold and items.
- Difficulty reaching long-range enemies.
- Vulnerability when heavily outnumbered.

> Fighters are strongest when they can stay close enough to an enemy to continue fighting.

These qualities become especially important during [[04-Dueling-and-Skirmishing|Dueling and Skirmishing]].
