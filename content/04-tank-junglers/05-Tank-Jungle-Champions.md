# Tank Jungle Champions

Several champions can demonstrate the tank jungle playstyle.

Examples include:

- Amumu
- Sejuani
- Zac
- Rammus
- Nunu & Willump

These champions commonly have strong crowd-control or engagement abilities.
## Common Tank Features

Tank junglers often have:

- High health or defenses.
- Crowd control.
- Engagement abilities.
- Defensive abilities.
- Strong team-fighting tools.

Each tank approaches fights differently. Some tanks specialize in starting fights while others may provide stronger protection or control.

> Tank champions create value through control and durability as well as damage.
