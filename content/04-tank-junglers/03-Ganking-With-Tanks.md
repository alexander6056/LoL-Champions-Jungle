# Ganking With Tanks

Tank junglers are often strong gankers because crowd control can prevent an enemy from escaping.

A tank may not have the same burst damage as an assassin, but they can create time for an allied laner to deal damage.
## Ganking Process

A tank jungler should:

1. Check enemy position.
2. Check whether the allied champion can help.
3. Approach without being seen when possible.
4. Use crowd control at the correct time.
5. Remain between the enemy and their escape route when possible.

Crowd control may include effects that slow, stun, knock up, or otherwise restrict enemy movement.
## Working With Teammates

Tank ganks depend heavily on teamwork. If the allied laner cannot follow the engagement, the tank may not have enough damage to finish the fight alone.

> Tank ganks are strongest when crowd control and teammate damage are combined.

Successful ganks can create opportunities to secure [[01-jungle-basics/05-Objectives|Objectives]].
