# Tank Strengths and Weaknesses

Tank junglers are useful because they can survive dangerous situations and help control enemy movement.
## Strengths

Common strengths include:

- High durability.
- Strong crowd control.
- Good team fighting.
- Strong engagement abilities.
- Ability to protect teammates.
- Useful ganking tools.
## Weaknesses

Possible weaknesses include:

- Lower damage.
- Slower ability to defeat some camps.
- Dependence on teammates for damage.
- Difficulty defeating enemies alone.
- Less ability to quickly eliminate important targets.

A tank may successfully trap an enemy but still need teammates nearby to finish the fight.

> Tanks are strongest when their teammates can take advantage of the opportunities they create.

Their crowd control is especially useful for [[03-Ganking-With-Tanks|Ganking]].
