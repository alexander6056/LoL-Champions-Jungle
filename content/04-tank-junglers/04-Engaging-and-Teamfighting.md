# Engaging and Teamfighting

An engage is an action that begins a fight.

Tank junglers are commonly responsible for engaging because their durability allows them to survive after entering the enemy team.
## Before Engaging

A tank should check:

- Are teammates nearby?
- Are teammates healthy?
- Are important abilities available?
- Is the enemy team grouped together?
- Is an important objective nearby?
- Can the team follow the engage?

Starting a fight without teammates nearby can cause the tank to be defeated without gaining anything.
## Protecting Teammates

Tanks do not always need to attack first.

Sometimes protecting an allied damage dealer is more useful than entering the enemy team.

> A good tank knows when to start a fight and when to protect teammates instead.

This makes positioning one of the most important parts of tank jungle gameplay.
