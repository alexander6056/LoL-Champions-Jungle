# What Are Tank Junglers?

Tank junglers are champions designed to survive large amounts of damage while creating opportunities for their teammates.

Instead of depending entirely on damage, tanks commonly use crowd control and defensive abilities.
## Main Responsibilities

Tank junglers commonly:

- Start fights.
- Protect teammates.
- Crowd control enemies.
- Gank lanes.
- Help secure objectives.
- Absorb damage during team fights.

A tank may still deal damage, but damage is normally not their only purpose.

> A tank creates opportunities that allow teammates to deal damage safely.

Tank junglers still need the normal fundamentals found in [[01-jungle-basics/index|Jungle Basics]].
