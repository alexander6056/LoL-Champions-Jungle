# Mage Jungle Champions

Several champions demonstrate ability-focused jungle playstyles.

Examples include:

- Karthus
- Fiddlesticks
- Taliyah
- Brand
- Morgana

Different mage junglers may focus on different parts of the game.

## Common Mage Features

Mage junglers may have:

- Magic damage.
- Area-of-effect abilities.
- Crowd control.
- Ranged abilities.
- Strong jungle-clearing abilities.

Some mages focus heavily on farming while others provide stronger ganks or team fights.

> Mage junglers show how jungle champions can create pressure through abilities instead of basic attacks alone.
