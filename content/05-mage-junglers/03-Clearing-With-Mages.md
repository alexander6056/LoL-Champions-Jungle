# Clearing With Mages

Mage junglers commonly use abilities to damage several jungle monsters at once.

This can allow some mages to clear groups of monsters quickly.

## Ability Management

While clearing, a mage jungler should think about:

- Ability cooldowns.
- Mana or other resources.
- Current health.
- [[01-jungle-basics/03-Smite-and-Jungle-Items|Smite]] availability.
- The next camp.
- Nearby lanes.
- Upcoming objectives.

Using every ability immediately may not always be the best choice if an enemy could appear nearby.

## Efficient Clearing

Efficient clearing means defeating camps while remaining healthy enough to make another play afterward.

> A fast clear has limited value if the jungler becomes too weak to gank or contest an objective.

Clearing decisions eventually connect directly to [[06-jungle-pathing/index|Jungle Pathing]].
