# Mage Strengths and Weaknesses

Mage junglers may provide powerful damage and utility, but they often have to be careful about where they position themselves.

## Strengths

Mage junglers may have:

- Strong ability damage.
- Area-of-effect damage.
- Long-range abilities.
- Crowd control.
- Strong jungle clearing.
- Strong team-fighting abilities.

## Weaknesses

Possible weaknesses include:

- Lower durability.
- Dependence on abilities.
- Vulnerability when enemies get close.
- Dependence on proper positioning.
- Difficulty escaping some enemy champions.

Missing an important ability may greatly reduce the mage's effectiveness.

> A mage can be extremely powerful while still being vulnerable when caught out of position.

These weaknesses make [[04-Teamfighting-and-Positioning|Positioning]] extremely important.
