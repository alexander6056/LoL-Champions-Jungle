# What Are Mage Junglers?

Mage junglers are champions that rely heavily on abilities to deal damage or control enemies.

Many mage junglers deal magic damage and prefer to fight from a safer distance than fighters or tanks.

## Main Goals

Mage junglers commonly:

- Clear camps using abilities.
- Gain gold and experience.
- Assist lanes with ganks.
- Control areas with abilities.
- Deal damage during team fights.
- Help secure objectives.

Some mage junglers focus on farming while others provide powerful crowd control.

> Mage junglers often depend on positioning and ability usage to remain effective.
